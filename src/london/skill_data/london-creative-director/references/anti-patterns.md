# What London Rejects

## Design Anti-Patterns
1. **"Mid" branding** — "In consumer-facing markets, 'mid' branding is insufficient due to market overcrowding"
2. **Over-engineering** — "Avoid over-engineering or attempting to solve the problems of the universe"
3. **Generic stock photography** — Shutterstock, iStock, typical stock sites produce bland imagery
4. **DaFont** — explicitly banned for professional font selection
5. **Pinterest for moodboarding** — "algorithmic, trendy, showing what everybody else already saw." Creates "mood board noise"
6. **Canva templates** — bypass for packaging design. Use The Dieline instead.
7. **Dreadfully boring design** — soulless, safe, forgettable
8. **Algorithmic content curation** — leads to homogenization, kills originality
9. **Google Fonts as default** — when Fontshare has the same font, use Fontshare
10. **2026 trend violation** — "No more ultra clean gradient heavy AI-like visual." Moving to human imperfection.

## Strategic Anti-Patterns
1. **Trying to appeal to everyone** — "Monoculture is dead"
2. **Being merely "liked"** — "infinitely better to be loved and hated than simply liked"
3. **Chasing every platform** — optimize for ONE primary channel
4. **Listening to irrelevant feedback** — only your target customer avatar matters
5. **Being too broad** — ignore advice about "not being too niche"

## AI Slop Markers (from frontend-design skill)
- Inter, Roboto, Arial, Space Grotesk as defaults
- Purple-to-blue gradients
- Glassmorphism used decoratively
- Identical card grids
- Gradient text on headings (foil effects only, earned by material)
- White backgrounds for luxury/premium brands
- System fonts when distinctive fonts are specified
