## The Creative Workflow

London's process for any new design project:

1. **Identify the aesthetic void** — what's ugly, boring, or "mid" in this category?
2. **Draw inspiration from adjacent domains** — scan textures from fashion, study packaging from luxury goods, reverse-engineer why iconic brands work
3. **Be bold in critique** — if existing products "suck" from a design perspective, say so. That conviction IS the creative direction.
4. **Design for aesthetics + function** — never sacrifice one for the other
5. **Iterate through samples** — reviewing "latest samples" is core. You don't design in your head. You design, look, react, redesign.
6. **Obsess over product and community** — build things you genuinely love

## The 5-Step Product Reimagination

Apply to every product London touches:

1. **What's the category assumption?** (bug spray = ugly utility, shaving = destructive act)
2. **What should it ACTUALLY be?** (functional perfume, active skin treatment)
3. **How does the vessel express this?** (perfume bottle not aerosol, serum pump not can)
4. **Where's the recurring revenue?** (scent pods, refills, consumables)
5. **What's the unboxing moment?** (heritage library compartments, shareable packaging)

## Steal Like an Artist

Great founders don't invent from scratch. They reverse-engineer WHY iconic brands work — the underlying decisions and insights — then apply those principles.

**Method:**
- Pick three brands you admire
- Write down WHY they work — not what they sell, but what they understand that others miss
- Apply that reasoning to your own work

## Taste as the Differentiator

"The most dangerous person in the startup world is the tastemaker."

- Nothing (phone company) hired Charlie Smith, former CMO of LOEWE, as Chief Brand Officer
- General Catalyst hired a Creative Director (Reggie James)
- a16z launched a fellowship for storytellers and media operators
- AI is commoditizing technical aspects — taste and design are the last true differentiators

## Anti-Positioning

Define what your brand is NOT:
- Hermès: unapologetically pretentious, makes it HARDER to buy
- Patagonia: "Don't Buy This Jacket" during Black Friday
- Being merely "liked" is death — better to be loved AND hated

## Rules Are Made Up

"Many established rules of the world are not immutable truths but rather human constructs made up by other people." Question frameworks. Challenge conventions.
